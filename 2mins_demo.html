Project Overview

Hospital readmissions are one of the biggest challenges faced by healthcare providers worldwide. Unplanned readmissions increase treatment costs, occupy hospital resources, and often indicate poor continuity of patient care. The Patient Readmission Prediction is a ML prediction of readmission of patient within 30days. The main functionality of each page in this project is described below:

  • Analytics Dashboard: Helps clinicians and hospital administrators make quick, data-driven decisions by providing valuable insights into patient volume, age-wise and gender-wise distribution, along with a range of financial and clinical metrics.
  • Readmission Prediction :Using a Pre-trained Machine Learning model, the probability of readmission of patient within 30days is predicted along with appropriate recommendations.

📊 Executive Dashboard

High-value insights are organized across the following sections:
Executive Overview – Summarizes key metrics including readmission rate, average length of stay, average hospitalization cost, and patient age profile.
Patient Demographics – Presents the age and gender distribution of all patients.
Hospital Operations – Analyzes admission patterns across Emergency, Elective, and Outpatient (OPD) channels, along with patient distribution across General, ICU, HDU, and NICU wards.
Financial Insights – Highlights the hospitalization cost distribution across spending brackets and identifies the highest-revenue cost categories among Pharmacy, Lab, Room, and Procedure.
Clinical Risk Factors – Examines the relationship between clinical indicators like length of stay, Charlson index and 30-day readmission rates.
Readmission Insights – Compares 30-day readmission rates across age groups and comorbidity (disease) counts.

🩺 Single Patient Readmission Prediction

Prediction: The following Patient data will be collected :

  • Demographic details:Age, Gender, Length of Stay, Charlson Index - weighted average score based on diseases, Comorbidity Index - Number of diseases, Number of Procedures etc.,
  • Clinical history:HBA1C, Creatinine, Systolic Blood Pressure
  • Healthcare expenditure:Total Cost incurred along with bifurcation of Government-Subsidy and Out-of-the-Pocket cost.

Based on these factors, a pre-trained Machine Learning model will predict whether the patient is likely to be readmitted to the hospital within 30 days.

📤 Batch Processing

end-to-end pipeline for batch processing.
Instead of single patient, we want to predict readmission probability for a group of patients then batch prediction comes into picture. The workflow is as follows:

Upload→Validate→Clean→Predict→Evaluate

Batch Prediction: Upload a CSV containing multiple admission records to generate readmission risk for all patients in a single run. The pipeline automatically:

  • Validates the file against the hospital schema (columns, types, ranges, allowed values).
  • Cleans and standardizes dates, missing values, and diagnosis/financial flags.
  • Predicts the 30-day readmission probability for the entire patients.
    • (Templates and reference sheets are provided to ensure consistency and uniformity in data processing.)
